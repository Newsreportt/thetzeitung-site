Di Tzeitung — Di Zelbstshtendige Yiddishe Tzeitung, the Independent Yiddish Newspaper — was founded in Boro Park, Brooklyn, in 1988 by Rabbi Avrohom Friedman, who continues to lead it today as founder and editor-in-chief. Published every Friday in the Yiddish language, and known in English as News Report, The Yiddish Newspaper of Record, it has grown into one of the prominent and historic institutions of the Yiddish printed word in America.

Who We Serve

Di Tzeitung serves the Yiddish-speaking communities of Brooklyn — Boro Park, Williamsburg and Flatbush — as well as Kiryas Joel, Monsey, Lakewood and other heimishe communities throughout the United States. Copies are also distributed in Canada, London, Belgium and Eretz Yisroel.

Our Approach

Our stated mission is to bring the news with an impartial eye. Di Tzeitung never involves itself in internal community disputes. We believe our readers deserve clear, accurate reporting — and nothing more. In general American politics the paper keeps a measured tone, with the goal of maintaining good relations with local, city and federal agencies for the benefit of the community.

What's Inside Every Week

Main Section — General politics, American and world news, business and the economy, and news from the heimishe community.

Yiddishe Tzeit (אידישע צייט) — The beloved weekly supplement of history, memoirs and heritage: the Jewish towns of yesteryear, great personalities and the stories of generations past.

Chassidishe Tzeit (חסידישע צייט) — Chassidishe news from around the world, and the popular picture section featuring Rebbes, chassidishe courts, simchos and events.

Forty Years Later

Like every printed newspaper, Di Tzeitung has weathered rising paper and printing costs and the transformation of the media landscape. Through it all, it remains one of the enduring voices of the Yiddish-speaking community — and we are proud that you are part of it.
