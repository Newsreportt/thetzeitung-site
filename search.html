Search the papers

Every word of every page, back to the first edition — and exactly where it stands.

Type a word — a town, a family name, a writer, anything — and the archive says which editions it stands in, which section of the paper, and on which pages. The pages themselves are not shown here: once you know the week, the edition's PDF can be bought on the back issues page.

Opening the index…

The search reads the printed pages themselves. Older papers were set in fonts of their own and a page here and there cannot be read by machine, so a word may stand in an edition the search does not name. For help finding something, call 718-851-6607.
