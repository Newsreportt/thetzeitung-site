Knowledge of halacha is critical
to living as a Jew. In fact, the
Chofetz Chaim zt”l writes in his
hakdama to Mishna Berurah,
Chelek Gimmel, “It is impossible
to avoid Chilul (transgressing)
Shabbos if one has not learned
Hilchos Shabbos thoroughly.”
Daf HaYomi B’Halacha – the
largest daily halacha program
in the world – is a most
remarkable, practical daily
halacha program. Participants
not only learn Mishna Berurah,
but also learn and understand
halacha l’maaseh – the
rulings of the great poskim
on thousands of modern-day,
practical questions.
Join Daf HaYomi B’Halacha
today, and become proficient in
the Halachos of day-to-day life.
Optional monthly bechinas are
available, with stipends awarded
based on successful scores.
