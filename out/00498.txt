Maimonides Good Health Message #20

At the Maimonides
Cancer Center, you have
a true partner in
fighting breast cancer.
Nearly 13,000 women in New York are diagnosed
with breast cancer each year. It’s a disease that
does not discriminate – women of all ages, races,
ethnic and socioeconomic backgrounds can receive this life-changing news. It’s also a personal
disease that can take wives from husbands and
mothers from children. Over one million American
women have died of breast cancer since 1970.
Breast cancer is difficult to prevent. Only one drug,
tamoxifen, has been approved by the FDA to reduce
the risk of developing the disease. However, there
are some things you can do to reduce your risk of developing the disease. These include exercising regularly, eating a diet low in fat that includes plenty of leafy green
vegetables and fish, limiting alcohol consumption and stopping smoking.
Early detection is our best weapon in the fight against breast cancer. When the disease is diagnosed at an early stage, it can be treated the vast majority of the time.
Some basic steps are key to catching the cancer before it progresses – they can literally save your life.
All women over 30 should perform a monthly self-breast exam. If you detect a hard
lump in your breast that doesn’t change with your menstrual cycle, make an appointment to see your primary care physician or gynecologist for a more thorough examination. All women over 40 should also have a yearly mammogram, a test that can find
problems early on so that treatment can begin when it will be most effective. Mammograms save lives.
At the Maimonides Cancer Center, we combine the best medical minds with the latest
technologies for the diagnosis and treatment of breast and other cancers. My colleagues and I are deeply committed to a team approach to care, with collaboration
between all of our specialists. A team of cancer specialists reviews each new patient
case. That means your diagnosis and plan reflects the opinion of a group of experts.
Whenever possible, we pursue treatment options and utilize techniques that are
minimally disruptive to our patients’ lives. Cancer treatments can be debilitating,
which is why having a world-class cancer center right here in Brooklyn is especially
convenient and important.
In the fight against cancer, our goal is to not only take care of the patient, but to make
her feel cared for. We think every patient deserves to be treated with dignity, respect
and empathy.
If you find a lump in your breast, or are experiencing pain or discomfort, see your doctor right away. Should you require further consultation, we hope you will contact the
Brooklyn Breast Cancer Program at (718) 765-2500.
We hope you will never need our services, but if you do, there is now a place in Brooklyn
where you can find the most advanced and compassionate cancer care.
