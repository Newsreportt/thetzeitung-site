ORDER TO SHOW CAUSE
PRESENT:
HON. DAVID I. SCHMIDT
Justice, Supreme Court
In the Matter of the Application of
IRENE DINOV, Petitioner,
For an Order dissolving the
corporation known as M & R
BRIGHTON
LAND
CORP.,
pursuant to the laws of the State
of New York.
UPON READING AND FILING the
annexed Petition of Irene Dinov,
verified the 6th day of May, 2011,
the holder of the 100 shares (half)
of the outstanding and issued
shares of stock of M & R Brighton
Land Corp. entitled to vote at an
election of the directors thereof,
LET Respondents M & R Brighton
Land Corp., the New York State
Tax Commission, the New York
State Attorney General, Michael
Bronstein, Aron Bronstein, Diana
Bronstein, and Rose Bronstein
and any and all other interested
persons, or their attorneys, show
cause before this Court, at an IAS
Part Com 2 thereof, at the
Courthouse located at 360 Adams
Street, Brooklyn, New York 11201,
on the 29th day of June, 2011, at
9:30 a.m., or as soon thereafter as
counsel may be heard, why an
Order should not be entered
granting the following relief:
a) Pursuant to BCL §1104-a,
directing the dissolution of the
corporation M & R Brighton Land
Corp.;
b) Pursuant to BCL §1106,

directing the corporation, its
officers, directors and those in
control of the corporation, to
furnish the Court with a schedule
of all information, known or
ascertainable with due diligence
by them, deemed pertinent by the
Court, including a statement of the
corporate assets and liabilities,
and the name and address of each
shareholder and of each creditor
and claimant, including any with
unliquidated or contingent claims
and any with whom the
corporation
has
unfulfilled
contracts;
c) Pursuant to BCL §1104-a(c),
directing the corporation, its
officers, directors and those in
control of the corporation, to make
available for inspection and
copying by Petitioner under
reasonable working conditions the
corporate financial books and
records for the three preceding
years no later than thirty days after
the filing of the petition; and
d) Granting such other and further
relief this Court deems just and
proper; and it is further
ORDERED that a copy of this
Order shall be published in the
News Report once in each of the
three weeks before the return date
of this application, said publication
shall be effected and paid for by
the corporation pursuant to BCL
§1106 (e); and it is further
LET a copy of this Order be served
upon the corporation and upon
each person named in the Petition
who is not a Petitioner, the New
York State Tax Commission, the
New York State Attorney General
and all other interested parties, in
the manner prescribed in BCL
§1106 on or before May 27, 2011.
ENTER : Hon. David I. Schmidt
